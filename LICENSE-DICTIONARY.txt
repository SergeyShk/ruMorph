The dictionary bundled with ruMorph (rumorph/data/ru.dict) is built from:

- the OpenCorpora dictionary (https://opencorpora.org), revision 417258, licensed under
  the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported License (CC BY-SA 3.0);
- the OpenCorpora annotated corpus (https://opencorpora.org), revision 4580223, from which
  the P(tag | word) estimates are made and on which the context model is also trained,
  licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported License
  (CC BY-SA 3.0);
- nouns, proper names and adjectives of the Russian Wiktionary (https://ru.wiktionary.org)
  missing from OpenCorpora, by the Wiktionary contributors, from the dump of 2026-10-01 as
  extracted by wiktextract (https://kaikki.org; snapshot:
  https://huggingface.co/datasets/SergeyShk/rumorph-wiktionary), licensed under the Creative
  Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License (CC BY-SA 4.0);
- counts of word form annotations in the training parts of the Universal Dependencies
  treebanks, release 2.18 (https://universaldependencies.org), and the weights of the
  context model trained on their training and development parts, licensed under the
  Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License (CC BY-SA 4.0):
  - UD_Russian-Taiga (https://github.com/UniversalDependencies/UD_Russian-Taiga)
    by Olga Lyashevskaya, Olga Rudina, Natalia Vlasova and Anna Zhuravleva;
  - UD_Russian-GSD (https://github.com/UniversalDependencies/UD_Russian-GSD),
    annotated and converted by Google, by Ryan McDonald, Vitaly Nikolaev and
    Olga Lyashevskaya.
