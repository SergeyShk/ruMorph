The dictionary bundled with ruMorph (rumorph/data/ru.dict) is built from the
OpenCorpora dictionary (https://opencorpora.org), revision 417150, as compiled
in the pymorphy3-dicts-ru package version 2.4.417150.4580142, including its
P(tag | word) estimates from the OpenCorpora corpus.
